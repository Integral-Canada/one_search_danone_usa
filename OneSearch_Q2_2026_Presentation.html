One Search Methodology

How OneSearch works

OneSearch combines three signals into a single view of search performance, refreshed each quarter.

Organic search

Google Search Console

Real clicks, impressions, and position for every tracked keyword.

Paid search

Google Ads

Campaign clicks, spend, and qualified visits (QV).

Market demand

SE Ranking

Average monthly search volume for every keyword tracked.

Every keyword is grouped into a content territory (Brand, Product, Health, Recipe, etc.) so coverage and opportunity can be read at a strategic level, not just keyword-by-keyword.

Coverage = (Organic clicks + Paid clicks) ÷ Market search volume

This is the core metric driving every recommendation in this deck — it answers what share of real demand each brand is actually capturing, by territory.

03 Rules that frame the plan

  • HEALTH coverage is falling (-1.1 pp), driven by a 91% SEM collapse in the Probiotics sub-category (plus a related 65% drop in Composition: Probiotics — together 83% of Activia's total SEM decline). Investigate the cause (budget, Quality Score, auction changes) before touching that budget — don't assume it's a safe pause.
  • SEM QV reflects page-level attribution, not capped at a keyword's own clicks — validate case-by-case before using it for any pause decision. Pause-test keyword lists have not yet been built or validated for Activia.
  • Quality Score data is pending from the Danone team — not yet available for this brand; the QS fields above will populate once it's registered.
  • COMPETITOR territory has only 1 classified keyword — not yet meaningfully built out, unlike ID/Silk's competitor-term coverage.

04 Next steps

  • Investigate the HEALTH/Probiotics SEM collapse (-91% in Probiotics, -65% in Composition: Probiotics — 83% of Activia's total SEM decline) before any budget decision there.
  • Validate SEM QV attribution before building any pause-test list — see rule above.
  • Start with the 5 product sub-categories above: SEO momentum in Yogurts/Probiotic yogurt is real and working, keep investing.
