Understanding How a One Search Strategy Works

The One Search approach aims to optimize overall performance by combining organic (SEO) and paid (SEM) levers around the same priority keyword territories. The goal is to maximize coverage of search intent — branded and non-branded — while intelligently arbitrating investment between the two channels.

🎯 Identify priority keywords

Select strategic branded and non-branded keywords with high volume and high conversion potential.

🔧 Optimize on-site content

Technical fixes, metadata and editorial content optimization to strengthen organic relevance.

⭐ Improve SEM Quality Score

Align landing pages with search intent to improve QS, reduce CPC and improve conversion rate.

👑 SERP Domination

Secure top SEO + SEM positions on branded queries to maximize share of voice and prevent competitive capture.

🌱 SEO/SEM Arbitrage

Prioritize SEM where SEO is absent from page 1. Gradually reduce SEM as SEO reaches top 3.

📋 Production plan

SEM A/B tests on RSAs and LPs to identify winning combinations before scaling. SEO Quick Wins first (Title, Meta, internal linking) on high-conversion-potential keywords.

Data Analyzed & Coverage Methodology

The OneSearch audit analyzes 6 dimensions for each keyword and category, from demand through to conversion.

1 · Demand

Monthly search volume per keyword — reference baseline for measuring coverage and identifying opportunities.

2 · Impressions

Number of appearances in the SERPs (SEO + SEM). Compared to search potential to evaluate raw coverage.

3 · Clicks

Traffic generated from organic and paid results. Measures the real impact of ads and optimized pages.

4 · Conversions

Transformation of search traffic into actions (purchases, leads). Compares performance by category and channel.

5 · Coverage

Understanding actual search audience coverage, based on your SEM and SEO data.

6 · Cost

SEM advertising spend (actual Google Ads cost) and SEO valuation via market CPCs — to measure budget efficiency per channel.

Coverage is the central metric of a One Search strategy: it measures the share of the Search audience actually captured relative to total market demand. Its reliability relies on Google Keyword Planner volumes, which incorporate the actual seasonality of searches — providing an objective market baseline, independent of internal performance fluctuations. High coverage means the brand effectively occupies the Search territory across all surfaces.

SEO Coverage

Σ SEO Clicks / Σ Search Volume

SEM Coverage

Σ SEM Clicks / Σ Search Volume

OneSearch Coverage

(Σ SEO Clicks + Σ SEM Clicks) / Σ Search Volume

SEO Cost

Avg. CPC × SEO Clicks
Note on volumes: A keyword is counted in the SEO volume only if it generates at least 1 SEO click during the period. Same logic for SEM. A keyword active on both channels contributes to both volumes without duplication in OneSearch.

SEO ImpressionsNumber of times the URL was displayed in organic results during the analyzed period.
SEM ImpressionsNumber of times the ad was displayed in paid results during the analyzed period.
CTR SEOOrganic click-through rate — measures the attractiveness of the organic result (title, meta, rich snippets).
CTR SEMPaid click-through rate — measures the relevance and attractiveness of ads.
Conversions (Qualify Visits)Number of qualified visits attributed to the Search channel during the period. Includes SEO and SEM separately. A Qualify Visit corresponds to a session with significant engagement on the site.

Qualify Visit (QV)Qualified session with significant engagement on the Oikos site. Primary conversion metric used to evaluate Search channel performance (SEO and SEM). Replaces revenue in a non-e-commerce context.

Conversions SEO / SEMNumber of qualifying actions (purchases, add-to-cart, leads) generated via each channel.
Quality Score (QS)Score from 1 to 10 assigned by Google to each SEM keyword. Directly impacts CPC and Ad Rank. Components: expected CTR, ad relevance, LP experience.
Landing Page ExperienceAssessment of the relevance and user experience of the landing page relative to the keyword. Component of Quality Score.
